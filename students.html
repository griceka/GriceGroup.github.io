Students

If you are a DePaul undergraduate or M.S. student interested in research, please contact me. Research projects are available when space permits.

Who can join?

You do not have to be a chemistry or biochemistry major. Students from related areas are welcome if they have taken some chemistry, are curious, and want to learn.

There is turnover as students graduate or move on to other opportunities, and breaks can be good times to get started.

Possible research areas

  • Inorganic and organometallic chemistry
  • CO₂ reduction and catalysis
  • Strong-bond activation
  • Bioinorganic chemistry
  • Computational chemistry / DFT
  • Green chemistry

Interested in an M.S.?

DePaul offers thesis and non-thesis M.S. Chemistry tracks. See the official program information for current admissions and degree requirements.
